# Dummy Run

AI plays your users and tries your app before your customers do. Every finding comes with its evidence.

A dummy run is British English for a trial run. Ours is literal. A cast of fictional users signs in to a
preview of your app in a real browser. Each one does the job its role exists to do. They report what
broke and what confused them. They also report anything they could see that their role should hide. A
reviewer checks every claim against screenshots, logs and code before it reaches the results board.

The dummies take the hit so your users don't, and every impact is on film.
