# Dummy Run

Fictional users, played by AI, try to do their real job in your app before your customers do. Every
finding comes with the evidence.

A **dummy run** is British English for a trial run. Ours is literal: a cast of fictional users (the
dummies) signs in to a preview of your app in a real browser, does the jobs their roles exist to do, and
reports what broke, what confused them, and what they could see that they should not. A reviewer checks
every claim against screenshots, logs and code before it reaches the results board. The dummies take the
hit so your users do not, and every impact is on film.
